# TryHackMe: Security Engineer Intro

Room that gave me the Introduction to Security Engineering badge.

Asset management and inventory: mapping, categorizing and tracking digital assets (IPs, applications, ownership), because you cannot protect what you do not know exists.

Risk advisory and mitigation: security is not about removing 100% of the risks, it is about advising the business to evaluate, prioritize and mitigate or accept the risks depending on its needs.

Secure by design: building security into network architectures and the software lifecycle early gives the best return on investment.

Operational readiness: policy enforcement, exceptions, vulnerability management and tabletop exercises to be ready for crisis management and business continuity.
